The problem

AI is already recommending healthcare providers. What does it know about you?

When patients ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity where to go for care, AI doesn't search a directory — it evaluates whatever structured data it can find. For most healthcare organizations, that means scraped reviews, outdated listings, and marketing copy.

Without verified data, AI often leaves your organization out entirely — or misrepresents it. Wrong specialties, missing credentials, no mention of your 20-year track record or your Joint Commission accreditation.

The organizations that give AI verified, structured operating data are the ones it can accurately describe and confidently recommend. That's what a TrustRecord does.
