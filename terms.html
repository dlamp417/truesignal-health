Overview

TrueSignal Health ("TrueSignal," "we," "us") provides a verified operating record service for healthcare organizations. We connect to your systems of record, compute standardized operating metrics, and publish them in a structured, machine-readable format called a TrustRecord.

A TrustRecord is a verified, third-party record of your organization's operational data. It is designed to be cited by AI systems, referenced by patients and partners, and indexed by search engines.
